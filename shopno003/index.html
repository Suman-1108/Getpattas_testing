Safety First

The Get pattas - Safe Celebration Guide

Follow these essential safety precautions for a joyful & safe Diwali celebration.

DO

  • Always burst crackers in an open space clear of combustible materials.
  • Keep a bucket of water and sand nearby for quick safety measures.
  • Store crackers in a cool, dry place inside sealed wooden or cardboard boxes.
  • Supervise children at all times while lighting fireworks.
  • Wear long cotton clothing and proper footwear while bursting crackers.

DON'T

  • Never attempt to reignite a cracker that failed to burst.
  • Never light crackers while holding them in your hands.
  • Do not light fireworks near electrical transformers or gas cylinders.
  • Avoid wearing synthetic or loose flowing clothes near flames.
  • Never throw crackers towards people, animals, or vehicles.
